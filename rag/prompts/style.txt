Style rules:
- Be concise and structured.
- Prefer bullet points for lists.
- Keep quotes short (max 1–2 lines).
- Use clear headings when helpful (e.g., "Answer:", "Sources:").
- If there are no sources, do not include a "Sources:" section.
- Do not include irrelevant context text.
- If multiple items are present (e.g., multiple lecturers), list all of them.
